Happy one year anniversary of Colombo Chetty Genealogy's website launch! &#127881;
 Excited to celebrate this day by sharing a few more wedding photos with the community:
- <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/post/wedding-photo/josephine-adrm" target="_blank"  style="color:#0077CC;text-decoration:none;font-weight:bold;"> Wedding of Josephine Anandappa and Anthony D. Rodrigo Muniarem Perumal </a>
-  <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/post/wedding-photo/aloy-rita" target="_blank"  style="color:#0077CC;text-decoration:none;font-weight:bold;"> Wedding of Aloysius Anandappa and Rita Anandappa</a>
- <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/post/wedding-photo/bonnie-victoria" target="_blank"  style="color:#0077CC;text-decoration:none;font-weight:bold;"> Wedding of Bonnie Anandappa and Victoria Candappa </a>
- <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/post/wedding-photo/joe-joyce" target="_blank"  style="color:#0077CC;text-decoration:none;font-weight:bold;"> Wedding of Joe Anandappa and Joyce Perumal </a>
- <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/post/wedding-photo/joe-rose" target="_blank"  style="color:#0077CC;text-decoration:none;font-weight:bold;"> Wedding of Rose Anandappa and Joe Casie Chetty </a>

Together, these are the weddings of almost all of Anthony Xavier Anandappa and Anna Pieris Tavarayan's children: Josephine, Aloysius, Bonnie, Joe, and Rose. The only sibling missing is Mary Louise whose wedding in 1917 unforunately has no known photos; however, you can check out <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/post/families/philip-raphael-mary-louise" class="link"> her family photo</a>.
